1999
Founded in Bentota

Where Luxury Was Born on the Shore

In 1999, visionary hotelier Ranjith De Silva purchased a stretch of pristine Bentota coastline with a single purpose: to craft a resort that honoured Sri Lankan heritage while embracing the finest global hospitality traditions.

What began as a boutique property of 40 rooms has grown into a 150-room landmark — twice recognised by Condé Nast Traveller as one of Asia's top resorts. Yet the soul of that original vision remains unchanged: to offer every guest an experience that feels both magnificent and genuinely personal.

Today, under the stewardship of the second generation of the De Silva family, Serendib Grand continues to set the benchmark for luxury hospitality in the Indian Ocean region.
